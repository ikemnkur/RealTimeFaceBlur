📖 About Face Blur

Face Blur is a privacy-focused web application that uses AI to automatically detect and blur faces in real-time video streams and uploaded videos. It runs entirely in your browser for maximum privacy.

Whether you're a journalist protecting sources, a content creator respecting audience privacy, or an individual protecting your identity online — Face Blur provides a fast, reliable, and secure solution.

🔐 Privacy & Security

  • No Server Upload: All video processing happens locally. Your video never leaves your device.
  • No Data Collection: We don't collect, store, or analyse your video data or personal information.
  • No Tracking: No analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts.
  • Camera Access: Only requested for webcam mode. Revoke any time in browser settings.
⚠️ Important: No automated system is perfect. Always review output before sharing sensitive content.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video data sent to a server?

No. All processing runs locally via TensorFlow.js. Your video never leaves your device.

Which browsers are supported?

Modern Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Chrome generally offers the best performance.

Can I process videos longer than 2 minutes?

The recording limit is 120 seconds to prevent browser memory issues. Record in segments for longer videos.

How accurate is the face detection?

BlazeFace is highly accurate for frontal faces with good lighting. Side profiles or obscured faces may not always be detected.
