FAQ
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Click Manage Billing above to access the Stripe billing portal where you can cancel, upgrade, or downgrade your subscription at any time. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.
Is my payment data secure?
All payments are processed by Stripe. We never store your card details.
What happens when I upgrade?
Your plan upgrades immediately. You're billed a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle, then the new rate going forward.
Does processing happen on your servers?
No. All face detection and blurring runs locally in your browser using TensorFlow.js. Videos never leave your device.
